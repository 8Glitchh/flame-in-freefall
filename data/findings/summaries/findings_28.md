# Document Title: Flame Extinguishment Experiment (FLEX) Research Summary

## Executive Summary
This document synthesizes findings from the Flame Extinguishment Experiment (FLEX, FLEX-2, FLEX-2J, and FLEX-ICE-GA) investigations into liquid fuel droplet combustion, flame extinction, and flame propagation in microgravity and reduced-pressure environments [31a87e259b21, a94667ef2b66, 65c1dc6d3142, 71a9df7eacbf]. The research evaluates single droplets, fuel mixtures, binary droplet arrays, and surrogate biofuels across varied oxygen concentrations, ambient pressures, and convective flow conditions [31a87e259b21, 984429272d54, e758917bbc8e, 7a937977ad84]. The generated evidence supports enhanced spacecraft fire safety protocols, improved material flammability assessments, and robust combustion modeling for both space exploration and terrestrial applications [31a87e259b21, 984429272d54, e758917bbc8e].

## Research Overview
The FLEX investigation series utilizes the Combustion Integrated Rack (CIR) to examine fundamental liquid droplet combustion physics without the buoyant convection distortions present in 1-g [a94667ef2b66, 71a9df7eacbf]. Key experimental parameters and variables include:
- **Fuel Materials:** Hydrocarbons (heptane, decane), alcohols (methanol, ethanol, hexanol), and surrogate biofuel mixtures (50-50 n-heptane/ethanol and 50-50 n-hexanol/n-decane) [e758917bbc8e, 71a9df7eacbf].
- **Fuel Geometries:** Single droplets (2 to 5 mm initial diameter), droplet pairs, binary droplet arrays, and one-dimensional droplet arrays containing fixed and movable droplets on silicon carbide fibers [b7971a55eef5, a94667ef2b66, c0c82997d66a, 252d3d9492af, 65c1dc6d3142].
- **Environmental Conditions:** Oxygen mole fractions ranging from 0.10 to 0.50, ambient pressures from 0.5 to 1.0 atm, and controlled sub-buoyant convective flows [31a87e259b21, 984429272d54, fe004783f7d0].
- **Test Categories:** Pure fuel experiments, fuel mixture experiments, convective flow experiments, and binary/extended droplet array tests (30 to 60 separate tests per grouping) [7a937977ad84].

## Key Experimental Findings

### Single Droplet Combustion and Extinction
- **Microgravity Burning Behavior:** In microgravity, small fuel droplets (2 mm to 4 mm in diameter) burn one-dimensionally, forming a spherical flame resembling a large olive centered symmetrically around the droplet [a94667ef2b66].
- **Oxygen Concentration Effects:** At high oxygen concentrations (0.10 to 0.50 mole fraction), chemical times are small relative to characteristic flow times, causing droplets to burn completely rather than undergoing flame extinction [31a87e259b21]. In controlled gaseous environments, deployed droplets either burn to completion or extinguish at a finite state [c0c82997d66a].
- **Pressure Effects:** Ambient pressure changes between 0.5 and 1.0 atm do not significantly influence the overall droplet burning rate, though sufficiently low pressures directly influence chemical kinetics [984429272d54].
- **Fuel Characteristics:** Methanol (an alcohol with a fuel-bound oxygen atom) burns with a very dim blue flame, low soot production, and a small standoff distance, providing distinct radiative characteristics compared to the alkane heptane [e758917bbc8e]. Initial droplet sizes spanning 2 to 5 mm establish deviations from quasi-steady behavior [b7971a55eef5].

### Droplet Interactions, Arrays, and Convective Flow
- **Binary and Extended Arrays:** Binary droplet array experiments position two droplets along a support fiber for simultaneous ignition and burning observation [252d3d9492af]. The FLEX-2J investigation utilizes a silicon carbide fiber with 3 fixed ceramic-bead positions and 3 to 10 movable droplets to monitor unpinned droplet motions, velocities before and after flame spread, flame leading edge positions, flame spread limit spans, and group flame growth processes [65c1dc6d3142, 0d9387b180f8].
- **Convective Flow:** Tethered single droplets placed at fiber crosshairs subjected to known, controlled sub-buoyant convective flows simulate the movement of a quiescent atmosphere relative to the droplet [fe004783f7d0].
- **Alternative and Bio-Derived Fuels:** Surrogate green fuels, specifically 50-50 n-heptane/ethanol and 50-50 n-hexanol/n-decane mixtures defined by the Italian Space Agency (ASI), are tested within the CIR [71a9df7eacbf].

## Fire Safety Analysis
- **Prevention:** High-oxygen environments—such as those encountered during Extravehicular Activity (EVA) pre-breathing spaces—promote complete droplet combustion instead of extinction, establishing critical high-risk boundaries for spacecraft fire safety [31a87e259b21]. Testing diverse fuels (methanol, heptane, and biofuel surrogates) ensures that flammability limits and material selection guidelines account for varying radiative and burning behaviors [e758917bbc8e, 7a937977ad84, 71a9df7eacbf].
- **Detection and Monitoring:** Tracking unpinned droplet velocities, flame leading edge positions, and group flame growth along droplet arrays provides critical metrics for early fire detection and hazard mitigation strategies [0d9387b180f8].
- **Suppression:** Understanding pressure effects on droplet combustion chemistry supports spacecraft cabin depressurization strategies as a potential fire suppression mechanism [984429272d54]. Evaluating droplet extinction limits across controlled gaseous environments aids in defining containment and extinction protocols [c0c82997d66a].

## Gravity and Mission Applicability

### Partial-G and Microgravity Applicability
- Findings regarding oxygen concentrations and droplet extinction are directly applicable to reduced-gravity space exploration environments where atmospheric oxygen and pressure fluctuate [31a87e259b21].
- Pressure influence data supports spacecraft cabin depressurization scenarios in microgravity [984429272d54].
- Single droplet combustion baselines, convective flow tests under forced ventilation, binary/extended droplet array propagation, and biofuel evaluations directly address microgravity liquid fuel hazards, flammability standards, and group flame dynamics [a94667ef2b66, 252d3d9492af, fe004783f7d0, 7a937977ad84, 65c1dc6d3142, 0d9387b180f8, 71a9df7eacbf].

### Earth Applications
- Results improve large-scale realistic fire simulations, safety model development, and industrial spray combustion models [31a87e259b21, b7971a55eef5, a94667ef2b66].
- Data assists in modeling combustion and extinguishment of liquid fuels in industrial settings, reduced-pressure environments, transportation burners, fuel leaks, and fire propagation hazard codes [984429272d54, e758917bbc8e, a94667ef2b66, 252d3d9492af, fe004783f7d0, 7a937977ad84, 65c1dc6d3142, 0d9387b180f8].

## Key Conclusions
The FLEX, FLEX-2, FLEX-2J, and FLEX-ICE-GA experiments provide comprehensive baseline data on liquid fuel droplet combustion, extinction limits, and flame propagation across varied oxygen, pressure, flow, and fuel configurations [31a87e259b21, 984429272d54, e758917bbc8e, 7a937977ad84, 65c1dc6d3142, 71a9df7eacbf]. The evidence demonstrates that high oxygen concentrations prevent droplet flame extinction [31a87e259b21], low pressures alter reaction chemistry while leaving burning rates largely unaffected [984429272d54], and multi-droplet arrays propagate flames via complex group behaviors [0d9387b180f8]. These insights directly inform spacecraft fire prevention, detection, suppression strategies, and material safety standards [31a87e259b21, 984429272d54, 0d9387b180f8].
