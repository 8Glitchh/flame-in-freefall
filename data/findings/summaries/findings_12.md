# Investigation Summary: ACME-SFLAME Gaseous Diffusion Flames

## Executive Summary
This document summarizes findings from the ACME-SFLAME investigation (Document ID: `f4ae0b6ffb4c60f6`, Page 2), which examined the ignition and behavior of spherical diffusion flames in microgravity. The research evaluated nitrogen-diluted and helium-diluted gaseous fuel mixtures across varying oxygen concentrations and pressures of approximately 1 atm [164e9d5c3839, 93da3117a3ca]. These investigations provide fundamental data on microgravity combustion, flammability boundaries, and transport properties relevant to spacecraft fire safety.

## Research Overview
The ACME-SFLAME investigation conducted systematic gaseous combustion tests under micro-g conditions [164e9d5c3839, 93da3117a3ca]. The experimental matrix explored the effects of varying fuel compositions, diluents (nitrogen and helium), and oxygen concentrations on the ignition and behavior of spherical diffusion flames [164e9d5c3839, 93da3117a3ca].

## Key Experimental Findings
- **Nitrogen-Diluted Fuels:** Spherical diffusion flames were successfully ignited using nitrogen-diluted fuel mixtures—including ethylene ($\text{C}_2\text{H}_4$ with $\text{N}_2$), methane ($\text{CH}_4$ with $\text{N}_2$), hydrogen ($\text{H}_2$), and hydrogen-methane blends—in oxygen-containing atmospheres (approximately 21% and 30% $\text{O}_2$ with $\text{N}_2$) at pressures around 1 atm [164e9d5c3839].
- **Helium-Diluted Fuels:** Spherical diffusion flames were successfully ignited using helium-diluted fuel mixtures—including $\text{H}_2$, $\text{CH}_4$, and $\text{C}_2\text{H}_4$ blends—in oxygen-containing helium atmospheres (approximately 21% and 30% $\text{O}_2$ with $\text{He}$) [93da3117a3ca].

## Fire Safety Analysis
- **Prevention:** Understanding spherical diffusion flame behavior with various diluted fuels provides essential baseline data for establishing spacecraft fire safety limits and flammability boundaries in microgravity [164e9d5c3839].
- **Comparison:** Testing both nitrogen-diluted and helium-diluted gaseous fuels allows researchers to isolate transport properties in microgravity combustion, supporting accurate spacecraft fire modeling and the validation of combustion kinetic models [164e9d5c3839, 93da3117a3ca].
